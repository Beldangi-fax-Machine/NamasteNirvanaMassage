About

Founded by Nikesh Pokhrel, Namaste Nirvana is opening on Longwood Drive in South Burlington, Vermont. Nikesh grew up in the Beldangi refugee camp in eastern Nepal, where he watched hard labor and years of stress wear down his parents' bodies — and it set him on a path to become a massage therapist.

Every session treats the mind and muscles as connected: pressure is adjusted to you, the pace is unhurried, and the goal is always the same — helping your body put down what it's been holding.
